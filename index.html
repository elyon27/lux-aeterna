OUR PURPOSE

About Lux Aeterna

A quiet space for remembrance, rooted in the Christian hope of eternal life.

✝

In prayer, we remember.

Lux Aeterna is a devotional memorial experience from CrownMind Institute. It offers a place to record the names of deceased loved ones, light symbolic virtual candles, and offer prayers for their eternal repose.

The sanctuary is inspired by Catholic prayer for the faithful departed. Its purpose is remembrance and supplication, not the promise of a spiritual outcome: the departed are entrusted to the mercy and judgment of God.

Essential devotional features are intended to remain free. Sign in to keep your private memorials, photos, candles, and prayers available across devices. You can also use the sanctuary with records saved only in this browser.
