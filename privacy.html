STANDALONE PROTOTYPE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Scope. This policy describes the initial standalone Lux Aeterna website prototype. It must be reviewed and updated before a production launch or activation of Supabase or other services.

Information you enter

You may enter deceased persons' names, optional birth and passing dates, memorial tributes, and written prayers. The prototype does not create accounts, collect passwords, process payments, or submit these entries to a server.

Where information is stored

Entries are saved in your browser's localStorage on the device used. Data is not synchronized across devices. Clearing browser/site storage may erase it. Anyone with access to your device and browser profile may be able to view it. Avoid entering sensitive information or using shared devices.

Sharing and third parties

The prototype does not publish memorials or prayers to a public wall. No Supabase integration, analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or third-party fonts are included. The external CrownMind Institute link opens another website, which has its own privacy practices. If hosted publicly on Netlify, the hosting provider may process routine server logs such as IP addresses and request metadata under its policies.

Access, export, and deletion

Use About → Export My Data to save a JSON copy, and About → Delete All Local Data to clear all Lux Aeterna prototype records stored by this browser. Deleting a memorial also deletes its associated local candles and prayers. Browser storage can also be cleared through your browser settings.

Minors and other people

Please use discretion when entering information about others. Do not include confidential or unnecessary personal information. This demonstration is not designed specifically for children.

Future revisions

When private user accounts, cloud database storage, or any public memorial features are added, this policy will need to specify the responsible operator, contact method, legal basis, retention periods, sharing, security controls, rights, and deletion procedures accurately.
