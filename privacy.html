Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Information you enter

You may enter memorial names, optional dates and tributes, photos, prayers, and candle offerings. When you use an online account, Supabase processes your email address and sign-in information.

Browser and online storage

Signed-out records stay in this browser. Clearing browser storage may erase them. When signed in, your memorial data is stored in the Crown-Articles Supabase project and database policies restrict access to your account. Browser records and account records are separate. Sign out on a shared device after using an account.

Access and deletion

About → Export My Data downloads a copy of records in your current view. Deleting a memorial also removes its linked photo, prayers, and candles. Use the Delete Account page to permanently remove your online login and all associated Lux Aeterna memorials, photos, prayers, and candles. Any service using the same login will also lose access through it. In browser-only mode, About → Delete All Local Data clears this browser’s records.

Service providers

Supabase provides account and database services. jsDelivr serves the Supabase browser library. Hosting and service providers may process routine connection information. Memorials and prayers are private to your browser or online account.
