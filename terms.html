1. About Lux Aeterna

Lux Aeterna is a free devotional app offered by CrownMind Institute and operated by Bienuel C. Tenio. It lets you create private memorials for departed loved ones, save photos and remembrances, offer prayers, and light virtual candles.

The app is inspired by the Catholic tradition of praying for the departed. Each virtual candle is designed to burn for 24 hours as a symbol of remembrance and prayer. Using the app does not arrange a Mass, sacrament, or other religious service. We make no promise about spiritual outcomes.
